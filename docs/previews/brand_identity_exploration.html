5. Scalability & Licensing Verification

Small-Size Recognizability (16px • 24px • 32px)

Non-letter marks can suffer when reduced to 16×16px if they rely on fine line density. To guarantee optical clarity:

  • Dynamic Stroke Weight: Scaled up from 1.25px (at 44px) to 2.5px–3px (at 16px) to avoid raster vanishing.
  • Silhouette Dominance: In Concept 2 (The Cartesian Sieve), the solid top-right quadrant provides an unmistakable asymmetrical silhouette that is legible even when peripheral vision glances at a browser tab.
  • High Optical Contrast: 100% deep ink (#1C1917) against pure white/alabaster achieves a 16.4:1 contrast ratio, surpassing WCAG AAA standards.

Originality & Licensing Audit

Per standing contract and founder directives:

  • 100% Bespoke Code: All marks and motifs are pure geometric SVG coordinate vectors authored from scratch. No third-party vector stock, no icon font libraries, and no paid service assets.
  • Typography Licensing: Fraunces and Inter are published under the open SIL Open Font License (OFL), allowing unrestricted commercial and digital distribution with zero licensing exposure.
  • Zero Trademark Conflicts: The proposed geometric marks are abstract mathematical compositions tied directly to SIFT's 2×2 coordinate logic, steering entirely clear of saturated corporate lettermark trademarks.
